Smart Traveler Safety Checklist
  • Verify Weather Alerts: Always review mountain weather advisories before starting high-altitude passes or trekking routes.
  • Pre-Book Verified Lodging: During peak tourist months (Dec-Jan, May-Jun), book accommodation in advance to avoid surge rates.
  • Emergency Cash: Certain high-altitude locations like Ladakh or remote Nilgiri tea villages have intermittent ATM connectivity.
  • Hydration & Altitude: Stay hydrated and allow 24-hour acclimatization when traveling above 2,500m (e.g. Manali, Rohtang, Leh).
